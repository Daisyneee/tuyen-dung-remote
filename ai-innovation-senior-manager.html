Role Overview

We build scalable digital platforms and operational systems for global clients. We operate with a strong focus on product excellence, engineering quality, speed of execution, and operational clarity.

AI is becoming a default part of how we work, not a side project. We are pushing for consistent, high-value AI adoption across every team, building automations that remove manual grind, and running the internal systems the company depends on day to day. We need a leader who can turn that ambition into shipped, reliable outcomes.

As AI & Innovation Senior Manager, you own our AI and internal-systems function end to end and lead it hands-on. You

manage three areas - Enterprise AI Automation, AI Enablement, and Research & Special Projects - including IT operations that keep the company running. You report directly to the Special Projects Owner and have real authority to set the AI agenda, decide what to build versus buy, and manage the risks that come with scaling AI.

We are looking for an AI & Innovation Senior Manager to elevate our data function from reactive reporting to proactive,

strategic decision-support. This is a high-autonomy role for someone who can own the full data lifecycle-from ingesting raw data via APIs and pipelines, to building robust ETL/ELT workflows, to surfacing insights through executive dashboards and automation bots.

This is a senior role. You are expected to lead people and still be close enough to the work to judge whether an automation is sound and an AI use case is real value, not waste.

Key Responsibilities

AI Strategy, Adoption & Innovation

  • Own the AI roadmap across Enterprise AI Automation, AI Enablement, and Research & Special Projects - decide what to prioritize, build, or drop.
  • Drive the AI-First adoption program - move the company from partial to consistent weekly adoption, and from casual use to genuinely productive, higher-value use.
  • Own enterprise AI automation - identify high-grind, high-volume processes and ship automations that remove them, with measured hours saved.
  • Run Research & Special Projects - evaluate new tools and approaches, run controlled pilots, and turn what works into rollout.
  • Build the systems and playbooks that let AI capability scale across the company as the team grows.

Data Architecture & Pipeline Engineering

  • Design, build, and maintain scalable ETL/ELT pipelines using Airflow, dbt, BigQuery, Spark, and Kafka.
  • Move beyond ad-hoc queries: Architect sustainable data models that serve multiple downstream use cases (dashboards, ML models, internal tools).
  • Optimize query performance and pipeline efficiency for large datasets.
  • Implement data validation frameworks to ensure 99.9% accuracy and reliability.

Automation, Internal Tools & IT Operations

  • Develop robust Python scripts, internal bots, and API integrations to automate manual operational workflows.
  • Identify repetitive tasks across the organization and eliminate them through scalable automation solutions.
  • Build and maintain internal tools that empower non-technical stakeholders to self-serve data.
  • Keep internal IT and systems running reliably - access, tooling, and operational support the company depends on.

Advanced Analytics & Visualization

  • Design and deliver executive-level dashboards (Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio) that drive strategic decision-making.
  • Apply statistical analysis to uncover trends, anomalies, and opportunities.
  • Translate complex data into clear, actionable business narratives.

Team Leadership, Mentorship & Standards

  • Lead, develop, and grow the team: AI engineers and analysts across automation, enablement, and IT operations.
  • Mentor and upskill junior Operations Analysts and Data Engineers.
  • Establish and enforce coding standards, documentation practices, and data governance policies.
  • Lead code reviews and technical design sessions.

Stakeholder Partnership & Thought Leadership

  • Partner with Data Analytics, Product, and Security on shared platforms, data access, and where AI touches sensitive data.
  • Partner with department heads and C-level executives to translate business goals into data initiatives.
  • Serve as the go-to subject matter expert for the organization's "Questions Channel," providing validated, context-rich responses.
  • Proactively identify data gaps, tooling needs, and process improvements before they are requested.

What You Will Be Accountable For

  • Set the standard for safe, high-quality AI use - manage the real risks (low-quality output, data security, over-reliance) deliberately as adoption scales.
  • Report adoption, automation impact, and risk to management in clear, business-level terms - hours saved and value created, not activity.

Strategic Decision-Making

  • Own technical decisions: tool selection, architecture choices, and prioritization of data initiatives.
  • Escalate only critical risks; resolve ambiguity independently.
  • Contribute to the quarterly and annual data roadmap.

Decision Authority

  • Full ownership of technical design for assigned domains.
  • Recommend and implement new tools, vendors, and approaches.
  • Define team best practices and documentation standards.
  • Influence the broader data strategy in collaboration with leadership.

What We Value

  • High ownership mindset and strong attention to detail.
  • Strong prioritization and decision-making under ambiguity, with explicit trade-offs.
  • Clear communicator - translates technical capability into business value for leadership.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • 6+ years in AI/ML, automation, data, or technology delivery, with at least 2-3 years leading a team.
  • Hands-on enough to judge the work: understands LLMs and modern AI tooling, automation platforms, and how to wire systems together - able to tell a real solution from a demo.
  • Track record of shipping automations or AI use cases into production with measurable impact, not just proofs of concept.
  • Experience driving adoption and change across non-technical teams - you can make a capability stick, not just launch it.
  • Sound judgement on AI risk - output quality, data security, and where not to use AI.
  • Comfortable owning or overseeing internal IT operations.
  • Communicates well in English.
  • Experience in high-traffic consumer platforms, high-value transaction environments, or fintech is a strong plus.
  • Experience building a function or capability from early stage is a strong plus.
  • Bachelor's degree (any discipline).
  • Core Languages: Expert SQL and Python (data manipulation, API integrations, web scraping, automation)
  • Data Stack: Deep hands-on experience with Airflow, dbt, BigQuery, Spark, Kafka (or equivalent)
  • Visualization: Advanced Power BI, Tableau, or Looker Studio (dashboard architecture, not just chart-building)
  • Automation: Proven experience building internal bots, Google Apps Script, or AI-powered automation
  • Data Engineering: Strong ETL/ELT design, data modeling, and pipeline optimization
  • Statistical/ML: Working knowledge of statistical analysis and ability to collaborate with ML Engineers
  • Soft Skills: Strong stakeholder management, proactive communication, and mentorship ability
