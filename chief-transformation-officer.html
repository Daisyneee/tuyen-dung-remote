Chief Transformation Officer

Role Overview

We are expanding our senior leadership team and hiring a Chief Transformation Officer, responsible for leading large-scale change across the organisation. The role is fully remote and works across multiple markets. We look at demonstrated ownership and impact, not titles.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own the transformation agenda, programmes and measurable outcomes.
  • Lead programme leaders and cross-functional teams.
  • Manage change, adoption and risk.
  • Report progress and results to executives.
  • Use AI and data to improve decisions and execution.

What We Value

Ownership of outcomes. Independence without supervision. Structured thinking and sound judgement under ambiguity. Evidence-based recommendations. Everyday use of AI at work. Clear communication in a fully remote setting.

Qualifications and Requirements

  • Has led an established organisation or function with real, direct reports (title alone is not enough), at a company of meaningful size.
  • Has owned business results with P&L or equivalent accountability, with measurable improvement.
  • Works without supervision and uses AI in day-to-day work.
  • Fluent business English.

Management Track Add-On

  • 2+ years of direct team management, including hiring, performance and day-to-day direction.
  • Has owned team output and been accountable for the results of others.
  • Own team targets, quality and workload; develop people, resolve escalations and manage underperformance.

Work Set-up

Fully remote. Monday to Friday, 10:00am–7:00pm GMT+8.
