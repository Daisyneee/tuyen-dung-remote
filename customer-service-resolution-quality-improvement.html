Role Overview

At G4, this role independently owns complex functional outcomes, leads improvements and influences cross-functional decisions. The role owns team results, coaching, people development and performance management. The Customer Service - Resolution, Quality & Improvement role delivers prompt, accurate, and professional customer support through chat-based service channels and ensures customer requests are followed through to appropriate resolution. The role handles customer issues and escalations, maintains service quality and documentation standards, coordinates with relevant teams, and contributes to improvements in customer experience and operational performance. Appointment level will depend on demonstrated capability, ownership, judgement, and business impact.

Key Responsibilities

G4 Leadership & Impact

  • Lead complex functional initiatives end to end, defining outcomes, risks, dependencies and success measures.
  • Diagnose systemic issues using evidence and implement sustainable improvements with measurable results.
  • Influence stakeholders across functions and make sound decisions under ambiguity.

Customer Support & Resolution

  • Handle customer inquiries, complaints, follow-ups, and escalations accurately and professionally.
  • Ensure complex or recurring issues are properly documented, investigated, resolved, or escalated within approved policies.
  • Communicate promotions, benefits, platform procedures, and other approved information clearly and consistently.

Systems & Cross-Functional Coordination

  • Use backend and frontend support systems effectively and identify recurring system or workflow issues.
  • Coordinate with Finance, Tech Support, and other relevant teams to resolve customer or operational issues.
  • Maintain clear visibility of outstanding actions, dependencies, escalations, and resolution progress.

Quality & Continuous Improvement

  • Maintain adherence to customer service policies, SOPs, data-protection standards, and service-quality expectations.
  • Use service observations, quality findings, customer issues, and performance information to identify gaps and improvement opportunities.
  • Support knowledge-sharing, calibration, process improvement, and other initiatives that improve service quality, speed, and consistency.

What You Will Be Accountable For

Accountability varies by Job Grade and Career Track. Depending on level, this may include:

  • Accurate and timely customer support and request resolution within the assigned scope.
  • Professional complaint handling, documentation, escalation, and follow-up.
  • Service quality, consistency, and adherence to approved policies and procedures.
  • Quality and outcomes across an assigned customer service area, shift, or process.
  • Team, department, or wider business outcomes for roles with broader scope.

The underlying work remains broadly consistent across levels; what changes is the scope of ownership, complexity, judgement, influence, and impact expected.

What We Value

  • Customer-focused judgement and professional communication.
  • Accuracy, attention to detail, and disciplined follow-through.
  • Strong ownership and commitment to seeing issues through to resolution.
  • Structured problem-solving and practical decision-making.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced digital environment.
  • Collaboration, timely escalation, and continuous improvement mindset.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Demonstrated independent ownership of complex work, cross-functional influence and measurable improvement.
  • Strong analytical judgement, risk awareness and structured problem solving.
  • Relevant customer service, customer support, or related operational experience is preferred.
  • Strong written and spoken English with the ability to handle customer issues professionally.
  • Ability to use digital support systems and manage multiple priorities in a remote environment.
  • Strong problem-solving, organization, documentation, and stakeholder coordination skills.
  • Ability to work independently and maintain service quality during high-volume periods.
  • Experience owning complex cases, escalations, service improvements, or operational initiatives is an advantage.

Candidates are assessed based on demonstrated capability, ownership, and impact rather than previous job title alone.

Management Track Add-On

As a Management Track role, the following people-leadership accountabilities apply:

  • Own team service performance, response quality, productivity, and customer outcomes.
  • Manage service coverage, workload allocation, team reviews, coaching, and capability development.
  • Own escalations when the team is blocked or material customer-service issues arise.
  • Support hiring, onboarding, performance management, calibration, and development of team members.
