Finance Operations & Systems Manager

Full-time · Remote · Salary: negotiable, discussed at interview.

About the Job

We are a technology company building scalable digital platforms and operational systems for global clients. We operate with a strong focus on product excellence, engineering quality, speed of execution, and operational clarity.

As we continue to scale, we are building a lean, modern finance function from the ground up. We are looking for a Finance Operations & Systems Manager to build the systems that let the finance team scale with the business without adding headcount.

The Finance Operations & Systems Manager owns the finance technology stack end to end - the ERP, the automated workflows around it, the multi-entity data model and the dashboards leadership uses to run the business. You sit between Finance and our engineering teams, turning manual processes into reliable, automated systems.

This is a hands-on role for a systems thinker who can design the architecture and also roll up their sleeves to configure, test and ship it.

Responsibilities

  • Lead the implementation, enhancement and configuration of the finance and accounting system
  • Design the chart of accounts, entity structure, dimensions (cost centres, products, partners) and approval workflows
  • Manage integrations between the ERP and banks, payment providers, payroll, expense tools and our internal platforms
  • Act as system administrator - user access, master data quality, change management and documentation
  • Map end-to-end finance processes (procure-to-pay, order-to-cash, record-to-report, intercompany) and remove manual steps
  • Automate feasible finance and accounting tasks such as reconciliations, recurring journals, invoice processing, payment runs and intercompany billing
  • Work with the Controller to embed internal controls directly into systems and workflows
  • Build and maintain self-serve dashboards for management - P&L by entity and business line, cash, revenue and unit economics - and deliver automated monthly reporting packs
  • Define finance KPIs and keep a single source of truth for financial data
  • Design the multi-entity data model - FX handling, intercompany mapping and consolidation logic - and partner with Data and Engineering teams on pipelines into the finance data warehouse
  • Ensure data accuracy, lineage and audit-readiness

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance or a related field
  • 5+ years in finance systems, finance operations, FP&A or a finance transformation role
  • Hands-on experience implementing or administering a cloud ERP (e.g. NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, SAP Business One, Odoo, Xero)
  • Strong understanding of accounting processes - general ledger, AP/AR, reconciliations, intercompany and consolidation
  • Experience designing data models for multi-entity, multi-currency reporting
  • Experience in a tech, SaaS, gaming or digital marketing company is a plus
  • Familiarity with data warehouses (BigQuery, Snowflake, PostgreSQL) and dbt or similar tools is a plus
  • Accounting qualification (CPA, ACCA) or ERP certification is a plus
  • Fluent written and spoken English

What Success Looks Like

  • The ERP is fully implemented, well configured and trusted as the system of record
  • Manual finance work is steadily replaced by automated, reliable workflows
  • Month-end close gets faster, supported by automated reconciliations and journals
  • Leadership has self-serve dashboards with up-to-date visibility of P&L, cash and unit economics
