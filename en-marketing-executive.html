External Marketing Executive

Full-time · Remote · Salary: negotiable, discussed at interview.

Overview

Our department manages a large network of external marketing vendors and supports acquisition marketing operations through reporting, process management, and day to day operational coordination.

The role is not focused on creative marketing. The team focuses on ensuring strong operational execution, clear reporting, effective vendor coordination, and continuous improvement of processes and workflows.

We are looking for individuals who are structured, dependable, proactive, and strong in operations, with the ability to take ownership, communicate clearly, solve problems, and contribute to improving how the team operates.

Executive / Senior Executive / Lead Executive

This role is responsible for day to day vendor communication and operational execution. The role is responsible for ensuring vendor related activities are properly coordinated, required information is accurate and up to date, operational tasks are completed on time, and issues are properly followed up until resolution.

Core Responsibilities

  • Communicate and coordinate with vendors on daily operational matters
  • Follow up with vendors on pending requests, information, documents, submissions, and action items
  • Collect, verify, and maintain accurate vendor and operational information in Radar, trackers, and other required system
  • Ensure vendor related activities are completed according to established processes and timelines
  • Identify missing, incorrect, or abnormal information and take the appropriate follow up action
  • Follow established SOPs, workflows, and escalation procedures
  • Coordinate with relevant internal departments when vendor related issues require additional support
  • Provide clear updates on ongoing issues, pending matters, and completed actions

Requirements

  • MUST do shift work (rotation shift work is required)
  • MUST be able to speak / write in both English and Chinese (able to communicate effectively in both written and spoken English and Chinese)
  • Good attention to detail and ability to maintain accurate information
  • Responsible and work independently in completing assigned work
  • Willing to ask questions and clarify when information is unclear
  • Proactive in following up on outstanding matters without repeated reminders
  • Open to feedback and willing to continuously learn and improve
