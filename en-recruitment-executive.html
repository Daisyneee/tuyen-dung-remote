Role Overview

The Recruitment Executive is responsible for the administrative and coordination activities that support the recruitment process.

The role focuses on maintaining accurate recruitment records, administering the Interview Hub and recruitment trackers, coordinating interview-related requirements, and ensuring recruitment information is complete and updated.

The individual is expected to work independently on assigned recruitment administration and proactively follow up on missing information or pending actions to ensure smooth recruitment operations.

Key Responsibilities

1. Recruitment Records Administration

  • Encode and maintain accurate candidate and recruitment records.
  • Ensure candidate information and recruitment status are updated promptly.
  • Maintain proper documentation across all recruitment stages.
  • Conduct regular checks to identify missing, incomplete, or outdated records.
  • Ensure recruitment data is organised and easily accessible for reporting and reference.

2. Interview Hub Administration

  • Maintain and administer the Recruitment Interview Hub.
  • Ensure candidate profiles, interview stages, interview results, and other required information are properly recorded.
  • Check that interview packs, feedback, transcripts, and other required documents are complete.
  • Follow up with relevant stakeholders on missing or incomplete interview information.
  • Maintain consistency and accuracy of information within the Interview Hub.

3. Recruitment Tracker Management

  • Maintain and regularly update recruitment trackers.
  • Ensure information across recruitment trackers and the Interview Hub is complete and consistent.
  • Monitor pending updates and follow up with responsible stakeholders as needed.
  • Conduct regular data checks to maintain recruitment data accuracy and completeness.
  • Support the preparation of recruitment data for reporting purposes.

4. Interview & Recruitment Coordination

  • Support interview scheduling and coordination between candidates and interviewers.
  • Send interview details and other required information to relevant parties.
  • Monitor interview schedules and follow up on pending interview feedback.
  • Support candidate movement across different recruitment stages.
  • Assist with other recruitment coordination requirements as needed.

5. Recruitment Administrative Support

  • Provide administrative support to the recruitment team and headhunters as required.
  • Assist in organising recruitment documents, candidate information, and hiring-related records.
  • Support recruitment reporting by ensuring source data is complete and accurate.
  • Assist with recruitment process improvements, templates, trackers, and administrative tools.
  • Perform other recruitment coordination and administrative duties as assigned.

What You Will Be Accountable For

  • Accuracy and completeness of recruitment records.
  • Timely encoding and updating of candidate information.
  • Proper administration and maintenance of the Interview Hub.
  • Completeness and consistency of recruitment trackers.
  • Timely follow-up on missing recruitment information and documentation.
  • Reliable administrative and coordination support to the recruitment team.

What We Value

We are looking for individuals who demonstrate:

  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Good organisational and administrative skills.
  • Strong follow-through and sense of responsibility.
  • Ability to manage repetitive and detailed information consistently.
  • Ability to identify missing or inconsistent information proactively.
  • Good communication and coordination skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities.
  • Ability to work independently with limited supervision.
  • Adaptability in a fast-paced and dynamic environment.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree or diploma in Human Resources, Psychology, Business Administration, Management, or a related field.
  • Experience in recruitment administration, HR administration, coordination, data entry, or a related function is preferred.
  • Strong attention to detail and data accuracy.
  • Good organisational and time-management skills.
  • Comfortable working with recruitment systems, trackers, and large volumes of candidate information.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, particularly Excel/Google Sheets.
  • Experience using recruitment databases, applicant tracking systems, or similar platforms is an advantage.
  • Ability to work independently in a remote environment.

G2 Success Profile

A successful G2 Recruitment Executive should be able to demonstrate:

  • “I independently maintain accurate and complete recruitment records.”
  • “I ensure the Interview Hub and recruitment trackers are always updated.”
  • “I proactively identify and follow up on missing information.”
  • “I keep recruitment documentation organised and reliable.”
  • “I provide timely coordination support to keep the recruitment process moving.”
  • “I look for ways to improve the accuracy and efficiency of recruitment administration.”

The defining expectation for G2 is independent task ownership, accuracy, reliability, and continuous improvement.
