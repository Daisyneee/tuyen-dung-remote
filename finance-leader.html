Finance Leader

Role Overview

A Singapore-based company is expanding its finance leadership team and is hiring Finance Leaders from Senior Finance Manager to Head of Finance. You will own the accuracy, integrity and usefulness of the company's financial numbers, and help leadership make better decisions with them. The role is fully remote and works across multiple countries.

Level, title and track (Expert or Management) are confirmed during hiring, based on the scope of outcomes you have demonstrated. Management Track roles carry formal people-management accountability. Expert Track roles do not.

Key Responsibilities

Financial reporting and close

  • Own the monthly, quarterly and annual close, financial statements and management reporting across entities.
  • Deliver clear variance analysis and decision-ready updates to senior leadership.

Planning and cash

  • Lead budgeting, forecasting and cash-flow planning, and challenge assumptions with data.
  • Turn business plans into financial targets and track delivery against them.

Controls, audit and compliance

  • Design, document and maintain the internal control framework; identify gaps and drive fixes.
  • Coordinate external audits and statutory, tax and regulatory requirements across jurisdictions.

Systems, process and AI

  • Improve finance processes, reconciliations and reporting through automation and AI tools.
  • Define KPIs and data sources, and work with data and operations teams to keep numbers reliable.

Influence and leadership

  • Advise non-finance stakeholders and simplify complex financial topics.
  • Management Track: hire, develop and direct the finance team (see add-on).

What You Will Be Accountable For

Accountability varies by grade and track. Depending on level: accurate and timely close and reporting; quality of forecasts and cash visibility; effectiveness of controls; audit readiness; measurable process improvement; finance outcomes across departments or the whole company.

What We Value

Ownership of outcomes. Independence without supervision. Structured thinking and sound judgement under ambiguity. Evidence-based recommendations. Everyday use of AI at work. Clear communication with leaders in a fully remote setting.

Qualifications and Requirements

  • Demonstrated ownership of a financial outcome, not only task-level contribution.
  • Progressive finance, accounting or FP&A experience with team-level or higher scope; direct team leadership for Management Track.
  • Strong knowledge of IFRS or equivalent standards, ERP and financial reporting tools, and advanced Excel.
  • Works without supervision and uses AI in day-to-day work.
  • Professional qualification (CPA, ACCA, CA or equivalent) is advantageous.
  • Fluent business English.
  • We assess demonstrated capability, ownership and impact rather than previous job title alone.

Management Track Add-On

  • 2+ years of direct team management, including hiring, performance and day-to-day direction.
  • Has owned team output and been accountable for the results of others.
  • Own team targets, quality and workload; run operating rhythms (1-on-1s, reviews); develop people and manage underperformance.

Work Set-up

Fully remote. Monday to Friday, 10:00am–7:00pm GMT+8. Availability for close and audit deadlines when required.
