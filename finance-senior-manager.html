Role Overview

At G4, this role independently owns complex functional outcomes, leads improvements and influences cross-functional decisions. This Management Track position includes accountability for team results, coaching, performance management and development.

The Finance Senior Manager contributes to financial operations, reporting, planning, controls, and finance transformation across multiple entities. The role manages the day-to-day work of a team, allocates priorities, monitors delivery, provides performance feedback, and develops team members while remaining accountable for team results.

This role carries formal people-management accountability, including work allocation, performance feedback, coaching, development, and responsibility for team outcomes.

Key Responsibilities

G4 Leadership & Impact

  • Lead complex functional initiatives end to end, defining outcomes, risks, dependencies and success measures.
  • Diagnose systemic issues using evidence and implement sustainable improvements with measurable results.
  • Influence stakeholders across functions and make sound decisions under ambiguity.

Financial Accounting & Reporting

  • Plan, assign, and supervise team work to prepare and review financial statements and management reports.
  • Review team output, remove delivery obstacles, and provide feedback to ensure the team can support account setup, consolidation, and multi-entity reporting.
  • Coach team members and improve team routines to maintain accurate month-end records and reconciliations.

Planning & Business Insight

  • Plan, assign, and supervise team work to prepare budgets, forecasts, cash-flow analysis, and performance commentary.
  • Review team output, remove delivery obstacles, and provide feedback to ensure the team can analyse financial risks and business drivers.
  • Coach team members and improve team routines to support management decisions with clear financial insight.

Governance & Risk

  • Plan, assign, and supervise team work to apply accounting policies, internal controls, and reporting standards.
  • Review team output, remove delivery obstacles, and provide feedback to ensure the team can monitor foreign-exchange and digital-asset accounting matters.
  • Coach team members and improve team routines to identify control gaps and coordinate remediation.

Finance Systems & Transformation

  • Plan, assign, and supervise team work to improve finance processes, reporting structures, and automation.
  • Review team output, remove delivery obstacles, and provide feedback to ensure the team can support transition from spreadsheet-based records to accounting systems.
  • Coach team members and improve team routines to coordinate finance requirements with Operations, Commercial, and Executive teams.

What You Will Be Accountable For

  • Team performance, service quality, and employee development.
  • Financial Accounting & Reporting outcomes at the level of team outcomes.
  • Planning & Business Insight outcomes at the level of team outcomes.
  • Governance & Risk outcomes at the level of team outcomes.
  • Finance Systems & Transformation outcomes at the level of team outcomes.
  • Fair work allocation, clear performance expectations, timely feedback, and development support for team members.

What We Value

  • Financial integrity and commercial judgement.
  • Analytical rigour.
  • Control and risk awareness.
  • Constructive stakeholder partnership.
  • Ownership, adaptability, and continuous improvement.
  • Inclusive people leadership and constructive performance management.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Demonstrated independent ownership of complex work, cross-functional influence and measurable improvement.
  • Strong analytical judgement, risk awareness and structured problem solving.
  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Business Administration, or a related field.
  • Professional accounting qualification is an advantage.
  • Strong accounting knowledge and experience with financial reporting.
  • Experience with accounting software and multi-currency environments is an advantage.
  • Demonstrated experience providing direction, performance feedback, coaching, and development support to employees.

G4 Success Profile

  • Anticipates risk, resolves ambiguity and owns measurable outcomes beyond routine execution.

A successful G4 Finance Senior Manager should be able to demonstrate:

  • Translates priorities into clear team assignments.
  • Monitors delivery and addresses performance issues promptly.
  • Provides regular feedback, coaching, and development support.
  • Takes accountability for team outcomes.
  • Consistently delivers team outcomes while preserving the professional standards required for financial operations, reporting, planning, controls, and finance transformation across multiple entities.

Management Track Add-On

This is a formal people-management appointment at G4.

Experience

  • Demonstrated experience supervising employees, allocating work, giving feedback, and coaching team members.
  • Ability to manage team output, quality, deadlines, and performance issues with appropriate escalation.

Responsibilities Added to This Role

  • Set team priorities, allocate capacity, review quality, and take accountability for delivery and outcomes.
  • Run regular one-to-ones and performance reviews; develop staff capability and address underperformance.
  • Support recruitment, onboarding, succession planning, and resolution of team escalations.
