Role Overview

At G4, this role independently owns complex functional outcomes, leads improvements and influences cross-functional decisions. Formal people management, team performance ownership and development are required.

The FP&A Senior Manager contributes to planning, forecasting, performance analysis, and decision support. The role manages the FP&A team's planning and forecasting output, reviews complex analysis, develops staff, and leads cross-functional improvements through formal team-management accountability.

This is a Management Track role accountable for FP&A team outputs, performance, coaching and development of direct reports.

Key Responsibilities

G4 Leadership & Impact

  • Lead complex functional initiatives end to end, defining outcomes, risks, dependencies and success measures.
  • Diagnose systemic issues using evidence and implement sustainable improvements with measurable results.
  • Influence stakeholders across functions and make sound decisions under ambiguity.

Planning & Forecasting

  • Independently own delivery to prepare budgets, forecasts, and planning schedules.
  • Resolve complex cases and provide sound recommendations when required to validate assumptions and consolidate inputs.
  • Guide colleagues and improve team practices to maintain planning models and supporting documentation.
  • Take end-to-end ownership of difficult planning & forecasting cases and share practical learning with colleagues.

Performance Analysis

  • Independently own delivery to analyse actual performance against plan.
  • Resolve complex cases and provide sound recommendations when required to identify drivers, risks, and emerging trends.
  • Guide colleagues and improve team practices to prepare management insights and variance explanations.
  • Take end-to-end ownership of difficult performance analysis cases and share practical learning with colleagues.

Business Partnership

  • Independently own delivery to work with stakeholders to understand operational drivers.
  • Resolve complex cases and provide sound recommendations when required to translate business questions into financial analysis.
  • Guide colleagues and improve team practices to support fact-based decisions and follow-up actions.
  • Take end-to-end ownership of difficult business partnership cases and share practical learning with colleagues.

Reporting & Improvement

  • Independently own delivery to prepare accurate recurring management reports.
  • Resolve complex cases and provide sound recommendations when required to strengthen data quality and reporting controls.
  • Guide colleagues and improve team practices to improve planning models, reporting efficiency, and analytical methods.
  • Take end-to-end ownership of difficult reporting & improvement cases and share practical learning with colleagues.

What You Will Be Accountable For

  • Quality and results across the assigned area.
  • Planning & Forecasting outcomes at the level of team or assigned-area outcomes.
  • Performance Analysis outcomes at the level of team or assigned-area outcomes.
  • Business Partnership outcomes at the level of team or assigned-area outcomes.
  • Reporting & Improvement outcomes at the level of team or assigned-area outcomes.

What We Value

  • Commercial and financial judgement.
  • Analytical rigour
  • .Constructive business partnership.
  • Clarity in financial communication.
  • Ownership, adaptability, and continuous improvement.
  • Credible influence through expertise and evidence.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Demonstrated independent ownership of complex work, cross-functional influence and measurable improvement.
  • Strong analytical judgement, risk awareness and structured problem solving.
  • Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related field.
  • Relevant FP&A, finance, or business-analysis experience.
  • Strong spreadsheet and financial-modelling capability.
  • Professional working proficiency in English.

G4 Success Profile

  • Anticipates risk, resolves ambiguity and owns measurable outcomes beyond routine execution.

A successful G4 FP&A Senior Manager should be able to demonstrate:

  • Owns team deliverables, controls, and results end to end.
  • Resolves complex issues and escalations while supporting team decisions.
  • Leads the team through clear priorities, coaching, accountability and performance management.
  • Leads measurable team-level improvements and develops team capability.
  • Consistently delivers team or assigned-area outcomes while preserving the professional standards required for planning, forecasting, performance analysis, and decision support.

Management Track Add-On

This is a formal people-management appointment at G4.

Experience

  • Demonstrated experience supervising employees, allocating work, giving feedback, and coaching team members.
  • Ability to manage team output, quality, deadlines, and performance issues with appropriate escalation.

Responsibilities Added to This Role

  • Set team priorities, allocate capacity, review quality, and take accountability for delivery and outcomes.
  • Run regular one-to-ones and performance reviews; develop staff capability and address underperformance.
  • Support recruitment, onboarding, succession planning, and resolution of team escalations.
