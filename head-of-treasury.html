Role Overview

Head of Treasury, owning treasury strategy end to end: liquidity, custody, banking relationships and risk, across multiple currencies and digital assets. Fully remote. You set the treasury roadmap, build and lead the treasury team, and are the final point of accountability for cash safety and availability.

Hands-on experience with digital-asset treasury operations is required for this role.

Key Responsibilities

Treasury strategy and leadership

  • Set treasury strategy, policy and targets, and own treasury outcomes company-wide.
  • Build, lead and develop the treasury team; allocate priorities and own team results.

Liquidity and cash management

  • Own group liquidity position: cash visibility, funding needs and short/medium-term cash planning across entities and currencies.
  • Direct the cash forecasting and liquidity-management function (see Senior Treasury Manager, Liquidity).

Custody and digital-asset treasury

  • Own the custody framework for fiat and digital assets: wallet structure, key custody, multi-signature controls, and relationships with custodians and exchanges.
  • Set policy for digital-asset treasury holdings, settlement and movement, balancing liquidity needs against custody risk.

Risk and controls

  • Own the treasury risk framework: counterparty risk, FX risk, custody risk and operational risk.
  • Direct the risk, controls and custody function (see Senior Treasury Manager, Risk & Controls).

Banking and counterparty relationships

  • Own relationships with banks, custodians, exchanges and other financial counterparties.
  • Negotiate terms and monitor counterparty exposure and concentration.

Governance and reporting

  • Report treasury position, risk and liquidity to executive leadership in clear, decision-ready terms.
  • Coordinate with Finance, Legal and Compliance on treasury-related audit, regulatory and reporting requirements.

What You Will Be Accountable For

  • Group liquidity position and cash availability.
  • Security and integrity of custody arrangements for fiat and digital assets.
  • Effectiveness of the treasury risk and controls framework.
  • Team performance and development across the treasury function.
  • Executive-level visibility into treasury risk and position.

What We Value

Ownership of outcomes. Independence without supervision. Structured thinking and sound judgement under ambiguity, especially around custody and liquidity risk. Evidence-based recommendations. Everyday use of AI at work. Clear communication with executive leadership in a fully remote setting.

Qualifications and Requirements

  • 8+ years of progressive treasury experience, including direct leadership of a treasury or cash-management team.
  • Hands-on experience with digital-asset treasury operations: custody, wallet management, settlement, and exchange or custodian relationships.
  • Strong knowledge of liquidity management, cash forecasting, FX and banking relationships, across multiple currencies and entities.
  • Demonstrated ownership of treasury risk and controls at company-wide scope.
  • Works without supervision and uses AI in day-to-day work.
  • Professional qualification (CTP, ACT, CFA or equivalent) is advantageous.
  • Fluent business English.
  • We assess demonstrated capability, ownership and impact rather than previous job title alone.

Management Track Add-On

  • Demonstrated experience leading and developing a treasury or finance team.
  • Set team priorities, run operating rhythms, and own team outcomes.
  • Support hiring, onboarding and performance management for the treasury function.

Work Set-up

Fully remote. Monday to Friday, 10:00am–7:00pm GMT+8. Availability around liquidity and settlement events when required.
