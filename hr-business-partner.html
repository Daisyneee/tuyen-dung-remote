Role Overview

At G4 and above, this role independently owns complex functional outcomes, leads improvements and influences cross-functional decisions. For Expert Track roles, formal people management is not inherent.

For Management Track roles, formal people-management accountability applies.

This role may be appointed on the Expert or Management Track within HRBP responsible for supporting business leaders and employees through engagement, employee relations, and people operations while helping maintain consistent HR practices, a positive employee experience, and policy compliance in a remote organization. At G4, the role owns or materially influences team-level HRBP outcomes, complex case resolution, service quality, stakeholder coordination, capability building, and measurable process improvement.

The Expert Track is an individual-contributor path centered on HR expertise, employee-relations judgment, workforce insight, policy interpretation, structured problem-solving, and improvement without requiring formal people-management authority.

Key Responsibilities

G4 Leadership & Impact

  • Lead complex functional initiatives end to end, defining outcomes, risks, dependencies and success measures.
  • Diagnose systemic issues using evidence and implement sustainable improvements with measurable results.
  • Influence stakeholders across functions and make sound decisions under ambiguity.

Team-Level HRBP Delivery & Stakeholder Support

  • Own or influence HRBP service delivery across the assigned team, business unit, or operational scope, ensuring timely and consistent support to employees and managers.
  • Guide managers through employee lifecycle matters including performance, probation, promotion, transfer, retention, offboarding, and organizational change.
  • Coordinate with Recruitment and relevant functions to resolve workforce issues and support business priorities.

Employee Relations, Discipline & Risk Management

  • Handle or oversee complex employee-relations cases, investigations, grievances, disciplinary actions, dispute resolution, and sensitive employee matters.
  • Ensure case decisions, documentation, and processes are consistent with company policy and applicable local employment requirements.
  • Identify recurring people risks, escalation themes, or control gaps and drive corrective action within the assigned scope.

Engagement, Workforce Insights & Improvement

  • Translate employee feedback, engagement data, retention themes, and workforce trends into practical recommendations and action plans.
  • Lead or coordinate engagement, culture, change-management, team-effectiveness, and HR process-improvement initiatives.
  • Track outcomes and communicate risks, progress, recommendations, and measurable impact to relevant stakeholders.

Grade & Track Ownership

  • Serve as a senior HRBP individual contributor for complex cases, workforce themes, policy interpretation, and team-level improvement initiatives.
  • Lead cross-functional problem-solving, improve standards or controls, and translate people data into actionable recommendations.
  • Coach or guide peers through expertise while remaining accountable primarily for expert contribution rather than formal people management.

What You Will Be Accountable For

  • Consistent, accurate, and confidential HRBP support within the assigned scope.
  • Appropriate handling, documentation, and escalation of employee-relations and people matters.
  • Effective execution of engagement, employee lifecycle, and people-operations activities.
  • Reliable adherence to company policy, applicable employment requirements, and HR governance standards.
  • Measurable team-level HRBP outcomes, stakeholder support, issue resolution, and improvement.
  • Expert judgment, analysis, recommendations, and influence without requiring formal people-management authority.

What We Value

We are looking for individuals who demonstrate:

  • Sound judgment, confidentiality, and professional integrity when handling employee information and sensitive cases.
  • Strong interpersonal communication and the ability to build trust with employees and managers.
  • Structured documentation, attention to detail, and disciplined follow-through.
  • Practical problem-solving and the ability to balance employee experience, policy, and business needs.
  • Adaptability and ownership in a fast-paced, remote working environment.
  • Analytical thinking, credible functional influence, and the ability to translate complex people issues into practical recommendations.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Demonstrated independent ownership of complex work, cross-functional influence and measurable improvement.
  • Strong analytical judgement, risk awareness and structured problem solving.
  • Progressive HRBP or HR generalist experience with strong depth in employee relations, engagement, workforce analysis, policy interpretation, or organizational change.
  • Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related field is preferred.
  • Working knowledge of HR policies, employee lifecycle processes, confidentiality requirements, and HR best practices.
  • Strong interpersonal, communication, documentation, organization, and problem-solving skills.
  • Comfort using HRIS, spreadsheets, and digital collaboration tools in a remote work environment.

G4 Success Profile

A successful G4 HRBP should be able to demonstrate:

  • "I own or positively influence measurable team-level HRBP outcomes in my assigned area."
  • "I support employees and managers with sound judgment, clear communication, and appropriate confidentiality."
  • "I identify people risks, service gaps, or recurring issues and take or recommend practical action."
  • "I use HR expertise, data, and structured judgment to influence outcomes without relying on formal authority."
  • "My work improves employee experience, policy consistency, and business effectiveness at the level expected for my grade."

Management Track Add-On

Where the role includes formal people responsibility, additional requirements apply.

Experience

  • 2+ years of direct team management, including performance and day-to-day direction.
  • Demonstrated ownership of team output and outcomes; hiring and onboarding experience preferred.

Responsibilities Added to This Role

  • Own team targets, quality, workload allocation, performance reviews, coaching and development.
  • Resolve team escalations and manage underperformance through appropriate action.

Work Set-up

  • Fully remote | Monday-Friday | 10:00am-7:00pm GMT+8
  • Availability to support time-sensitive employee matters, investigations, organizational changes, or leadership needs when required.
