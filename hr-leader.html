Role Overview

A Singapore-based company is expanding its people team and is hiring HR Leaders from Senior Manager to Head of HR, covering HR operations, Compensation & Benefits (C&B) and HR business partnering. You will own people outcomes that leaders can see, and keep HR running accurately across multiple countries. The role is fully remote.

Level, title and track (Expert or Management) are confirmed during hiring. Management Track roles carry formal people-management accountability. Expert Track roles do not.

Key Responsibilities

People operations and compliance

  • Own employee lifecycle processes, policies, employment contracts and statutory requirements across jurisdictions, working with external advisors where needed.
  • Keep HR records, HRIS data and documentation accurate and confidential.

Compensation and benefits

  • Oversee payroll validation, reconciliation and compensation administration; investigate discrepancies and drive corrective action.

Business partnering and talent

  • Partner with senior leaders on organisation design, hiring plans, performance, retention and employee relations.
  • Support hiring, onboarding and capability development.

HR analytics, systems and AI

  • Use HR data and AI tools to generate insights, improve accuracy and reduce cycle time.
  • Improve HR processes and systems so they scale without slowing the business.

Leadership

  • Management Track: build, coach and lead the HR team (see add-on).

What You Will Be Accountable For

Depending on grade and track: accurate and timely HR and payroll operations; compliance and records quality; employee service quality; measurable people outcomes in an assigned scope, a department or the company; process and system improvement.

What We Value

Accuracy and confidentiality. Ownership of results. Independence without supervision. Practical problem-solving and sound judgement. Influence through expertise. Everyday use of AI at work. Clear communication with employees and leaders in a fully remote environment.

Qualifications and Requirements

  • Demonstrated ownership of an HR or C&B outcome you can measure.
  • Progressive HR, C&B or HR operations experience with team-level or higher scope; direct team leadership for Management Track.
  • Working knowledge of payroll inputs and controls, HRMS/payroll systems and labour regulations, ideally across more than one country.
  • Works without supervision and uses AI in day-to-day work.
  • Bachelor's degree in any discipline; HR, Business, Finance or related is advantageous.
  • Fluent business English.
  • We assess demonstrated capability, ownership and impact rather than previous job title alone.

Management Track Add-On

  • 2+ years of direct team management, including performance and day-to-day direction.
  • Has owned team output and outcomes; hiring and onboarding experience preferred.
  • Own team targets, quality, workload allocation, performance reviews and coaching; resolve escalations and manage underperformance.

Work Set-up

Fully remote. Monday to Friday, 10:00am–7:00pm GMT+8. Availability for payroll closing and time-sensitive employee matters when required.
