Role Overview

Our company is growing rapidly and expanding its leadership team. As Operational Coach, you'll coach leaders as they navigate increasingly complex business, leadership, team, and organizational challenges.

This is an operator-to-operator coaching role. It requires senior leadership experience and responsibility for business performance. Your role is to strengthen the thinking of our leaders, challenge their assumptions, and help them develop action plans they own (not you).

Key Responsibilities

  • Coach leaders through one-on-one coaching and other coaching formats.
  • Help leaders reflect, sharpen their thinking, identify blind spots, and grow as leaders.
  • Challenge assumptions through thoughtful questions while respecting task ownership.
  • Draw on your operating experience to ask better questions - not to prescribe answers.
  • Identify leadership and organizational issues and share insights with the C-Suite.
  • Operate autonomously, identifying where coaching can have the greatest impact.

What You Will Be Accountable For

You'll help build a stronger leadership organization that delivers better business outcomes.

Success includes:

  • More committed, capable leaders who make better decisions, take greater ownership, and live and breathe our values and frameworks.
  • High-performing teams with clear strategies, priorities, and measurable objectives that are understood by the team members.
  • Stronger execution that enables the business to build and maintain a growing portfolio of technology products.

What We Value

  • Desire to help executives reflect, identify gaps, and grow without telling them what to do.
  • Excellent judgment, communication, and relationship-building skills.

Qualifications & Requirements

Must have qualifications / skills

  • Recent experience leading large, multi-team organizations and managing managers.
  • Recent P&L responsibility or equivalent accountability for business-unit performance.
  • Track record of delivering measurable improvements in business performance.
  • Near-native fluency in English.

Nice to have qualifications / skills

  • Experience building teams and functions from scratch
  • A recognized coaching certification.
  • A relevant postgraduate degree.
