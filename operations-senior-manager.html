Role Overview

The Operations Senior Manager contributes to day-to-day operational execution, control, and service continuity. The role owns department plans, resources, capability, controls, and results; builds a sustainable team and aligns department delivery with wider business priorities. This role carries formal people-management accountability, including work allocation, performance feedback, coaching, development, and responsibility for department-level outcomes.

Key Responsibilities

Transaction & Workflow Execution

  • Set department priorities, resources, and controls to complete required checks and documentation.
  • Own department performance, risk treatment, and management review to meet agreed service timelines.
  • Plan resources, build team capability, and manage department performance in transaction & workflow

execution.

Monitoring & Handover

  • Set department priorities, resources, and controls to maintain clear status records.
  • Own department performance, risk treatment, and management review to handover unresolved cases with

complete context.

  • Plan resources, build team capability, and manage department performance in monitoring & handover.

Exception Handling

  • Set department priorities, resources, and controls to perform first-line investigation.
  • Own department performance, risk treatment, and management review to escalate risks through the

approved path.

  • Plan resources, build team capability, and manage department performance in exception handling.

Process Discipline

  • Set department priorities, resources, and controls to suggest practical improvements to recurring workflow

issues.

  • Own department performance, risk treatment, and management review to support implementation of

approved changes.

  • Plan resources, build team capability, and manage department performance in process discipline.

What You Will Be Accountable For

  • Department performance, resources, controls, and capability.
  • Transaction & Workflow Execution outcomes at the level of department-level outcomes.
  • Monitoring & Handover outcomes at the level of department-level outcomes.
  • Exception Handling outcomes at the level of department-level outcomes.
  • Process Discipline outcomes at the level of department-level outcomes.
  • Fair work allocation, clear performance expectations, timely feedback, and development support for team

members.

What We Value

  • Accuracy and reliability.
  • Ownership of assigned outcomes.
  • Clear escalation judgement.
  • Continuous-improvement mindset.
  • Ownership, adaptability, and continuous improvement.
  • Inclusive people leadership and constructive performance management.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Relevant education or equivalent practical experience.
  • Experience in operations, customer service, or transaction processing is an advantage.
  • Strong attention to detail and organisation.
  • Professional working proficiency in English.
  • Demonstrated experience providing direction, performance feedback, coaching, and development support

to employees.

G4 Success Profile

A successful G4 Operations Senior Manager should be able to demonstrate:

  • Sets clear department priorities and operating plans.
  • Allocates resources and manages department performance.
  • Builds capability, succession, and a healthy performance culture.
  • Owns department risks and results.
  • Consistently delivers department-level outcomes while preserving the professional standards required for

day-to-day operational execution, control, and service continuity.
