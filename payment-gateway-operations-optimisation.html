Role Overview

At G4, this role independently owns complex functional outcomes, leads improvements and influences cross-functional decisions. The role owns team results, coaching, people development and performance management. The Payment Gateway - Operations & Optimisation role supports payment gateway operations, transaction integrity, incident management, and partner coordination. The role monitors payment flows, resolves transaction issues, coordinates with payment providers, and improves operational controls and service reliability. This G4 Management Track role requires demonstrated capability, ownership, judgement and business impact, and includes formal team accountability.

Key Responsibilities

G4 Leadership & Impact

  • Lead complex functional initiatives end to end, defining outcomes, risks, dependencies and success measures.
  • Diagnose systemic issues using evidence and implement sustainable improvements with measurable results.
  • Influence stakeholders across functions and make sound decisions under ambiguity.

Payment Operations

  • Monitor payment flows, gateway service status, operational checks, and reconciliations.
  • Maintain accurate and complete transaction, incident, and operational records.
  • Take ownership of payment operations cases through resolution.

Incident & Exception Management

  • Investigate failed, delayed, mismatched, or abnormal transactions and determine appropriate action.
  • Coordinate resolution with internal teams and payment providers.
  • Escalate material incidents with clear evidence, impact assessment, and recommendations.

Partner Coordination

  • Manage operational communication and follow-up with payment partners.
  • Track provider actions, service dependencies, onboarding, and change activities.
  • Maintain clear updates on partner issues, actions, and outstanding matters.

Controls & Optimisation

  • Apply payment controls, operating procedures, and reconciliation requirements.
  • Analyse recurring transaction issues and identify root causes or improvement opportunities.
  • Improve monitoring, reconciliation, operational processes, and service reliability.

What You Will Be Accountable For

Accountability varies by Job Grade and Career Track. Depending on level, this may include:

  • Accurate and timely completion of assigned payment operations work.
  • Quality and results across an assigned payment gateway, process, or operational area.
  • Resolution of complex incidents and exceptions with limited guidance.
  • Team or department-level payment operations, controls, and service outcomes.
  • Cross-team or wider business outcomes for roles with broader scope.

The underlying work remains broadly consistent across levels; what changes is the scope of ownership, complexity, judgement, influence, and impact expected.

What We Value

  • Transaction accuracy and strong risk awareness.
  • Analytical troubleshooting and sound operational judgement.
  • Ownership, adaptability, and dependable follow-through.
  • Responsive and clear partner communication.
  • Ability to resolve complex issues using evidence and practical recommendations.
  • Continuous improvement and credible influence through expertise.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Demonstrated independent ownership of complex work, cross-functional influence and measurable improvement.
  • Strong analytical judgement, risk awareness and structured problem solving.
  • Relevant education in Business, Finance, Technology, or a related field.
  • Experience in payments, fintech, banking operations, transaction support, or a related environment is an advantage.
  • Strong numerical, reconciliation, and analytical skills.
  • Ability to communicate clearly in English.
  • Ability to independently manage operational issues and priorities.
  • Comfort working across internal teams and external payment providers.

Candidates are assessed based on demonstrated capability, ownership, and impact rather than previous job title alone.

Management Track Add-On

As a Management Track role, the following people-leadership accountabilities apply:

  • Direct ownership of team performance, targets, quality, and payment operations output.
  • Team operating rhythms, workload allocation, coaching, and capability development.
  • Ownership of escalations when the team is blocked or material payment issues arise.
  • Support hiring, onboarding, performance management, and development of team members.
