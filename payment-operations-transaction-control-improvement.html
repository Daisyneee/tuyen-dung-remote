Role Overview

At G4, this role independently owns complex functional outcomes, leads improvements and influences cross-functional decisions. The role owns team results, coaching, people development and performance management.

The Payment Operations - Transaction Control & Improvement role supports secure, accurate, and efficient payment operations across incoming and outgoing payments, transaction approvals, payment channels, reconciliation, and payment-related issue resolution.

The role monitors payment performance, manages exceptions and incidents, maintains operational controls, coordinates across teams, and drives improvements that strengthen accuracy, efficiency, and service continuity.

Appointment at G4 requires demonstrated capability, ownership, judgement, business impact, and direct leadership.

Formal team management and performance accountability apply.

Key Responsibilities

G4 Leadership & Impact

  • Lead complex functional initiatives end to end, defining outcomes, risks, dependencies and success measures.
  • Diagnose systemic issues using evidence and implement sustainable improvements with measurable results.
  • Influence stakeholders across functions and make sound decisions under ambiguity.

Payment Operations & Transaction Control

  • Support accurate and timely processing of incoming and outgoing payments, approvals, and related payment activities.
  • Monitor transaction queues, workload, accuracy, exceptions, turnaround time, and relevant payment KPIs.
  • Investigate, resolve, or escalate complex and high-risk payment exceptions with clear documentation.

Gateways, Reconciliation & Incident Resolution

  • Monitor payment gateways, banking channels, transaction activity, and reconciliation status.
  • Identify mismatches, instability, recurring issues, and payment-related risks.
  • Coordinate incident investigation and resolution across Finance, Risk, Customer Service, Technology, and other relevant stakeholders.

Controls, Reporting & Improvement

  • Maintain adherence to SOPs, operational controls, company policies, and approved payment procedures.
  • Analyse payment data, reconciliation findings, and operational trends to identify gaps and improvement opportunities.
  • Prepare reliable operational reports and lead or support improvements that enhance efficiency, accuracy, service quality, and control.

What You Will Be Accountable For

Accountability varies by Job Grade and Career Track. Depending on level, this may include:

  • Accurate, timely, and secure payment processing within the assigned scope.
  • Reliable transaction verification, reconciliation, documentation, and exception handling.
  • Quality and results across an assigned payment process, shift, team, or operational area.
  • Adherence to SOPs, operational controls, data-protection requirements, and company policies.
  • Team, department, or wider business payment outcomes for roles with broader scope.
  • Quality of analysis, recommendations, reporting, controls, and improvement initiatives.

The underlying work remains broadly consistent across levels; what changes is the scope of ownership, complexity, judgement, influence, and impact expected.

What We Value

  • Strong ownership, accuracy, and attention to detail.
  • Analytical thinking and structured problem-solving.
  • Integrity, confidentiality, and disciplined handling of financial information.
  • Calm decision-making in a high-volume, fast-paced payment environment.
  • Clear communication, cross-functional collaboration, and reliable follow-through.
  • Continuous improvement mindset and respect for operational controls.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Demonstrated independent ownership of complex work, cross-functional influence and measurable improvement.
  • Strong analytical judgement, risk awareness and structured problem solving.
  • Relevant experience in payment operations, banking, finance, cashiering, transaction processing, or a related operational environment is preferred.
  • Working knowledge of payment processes, transaction monitoring, verification, reconciliation, and operational controls appropriate to the role level.
  • Strong attention to detail, problem-solving ability, and comfort working under pressure.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office or Google Workspace, particularly Excel/Google Sheets.
  • Good written and spoken English and ability to coordinate clearly with internal stakeholders.
  • Ability to work independently while maintaining accuracy, confidentiality, and discipline in a remote environment.

Candidates are assessed based on demonstrated capability, ownership, and impact rather than previous job title alone.

Management Track Add-On

As a Management Track role, the following people-leadership accountabilities apply:

  • Own team payment performance, targets, quality, controls, and operational output.
  • Run team operating rhythms, workload allocation, handovers, coaching, and capability development.
  • Own escalations and ensure timely response to material payment issues or performance gaps.
  • Support hiring, onboarding, performance management, and development of team members.
