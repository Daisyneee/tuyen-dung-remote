Role Overview

At G4, this role independently owns complex functional outcomes, leads improvements and influences cross-functional decisions. The role owns team results, coaching, people development and performance management.

Payment Support serves as the Payment Gateway management and evaluation system, customer service centre, and early payment-situation signal data collection centre. Its objective is to support 24-hour payment uptime through different strategies, maintain customer resolution time in line with market benchmarks, and prepare the business ahead of potential payment-squeeze situations.

The role supports these objectives through disciplined documentation, customer and operational issue resolution, payment-service monitoring, cross-functional coordination, and continuous improvement.

Appointment at G4 requires demonstrated capability, ownership, judgement, business impact, and direct leadership.

Formal team management and performance accountability apply.

Key Responsibilities

G4 Leadership & Impact

  • Lead complex functional initiatives end to end, defining outcomes, risks, dependencies and success measures.
  • Diagnose systemic issues using evidence and implement sustainable improvements with measurable results.
  • Influence stakeholders across functions and make sound decisions under ambiguity.

Payment Service Monitoring & Early Warning

  • Monitor payment-service conditions, operational signals, customer issues, and relevant data to identify emerging risks or service disruptions.
  • Collect and maintain reliable information that supports payment gateway evaluation, service continuity, and early warning of potential payment-squeeze situations.
  • Escalate significant payment or service risks with clear documentation, evidence, and recommended next steps.

Customer Resolution & Cross-Functional Coordination

  • Support accurate and timely handling of customer and operational payment-related issues.
  • Coordinate with Payment Operations, Finance, Customer Service, Technology, and other stakeholders until issues are resolved.
  • Track response and resolution time, outstanding actions, dependencies, and escalations to support service levels aligned with market benchmarks.

Documentation & Operational Excellence

  • Maintain complete, accurate, and reliable documentation of requests, incidents, actions, findings, and resolutions.
  • Follow approved procedures, service policies, data-protection requirements, and escalation standards.
  • Identify recurring issues, workflow gaps, or control weaknesses and improve team processes, documentation, and ways of working.
  • Demonstrate strong task ownership and remain accountable for assigned work through completion or proper handover.

Grade & Track Ownership

  • Own or influence measurable outcomes appropriate to the assigned Job Grade and Career Track.
  • Use data, operational observations, documentation findings, and structured problem-solving to improve Payment Support outcomes.
  • Influence stakeholders through expertise, clear recommendations, reliable reporting, and disciplined process or project ownership.
  • Lead or support improvement initiatives from problem identification through implementation and impact measurement.

What You Will Be Accountable For

Accountability varies by Job Grade and Career Track. Depending on level, this may include:

  • Reliable payment-service monitoring and early identification of operational risks.
  • Timely customer and operational issue resolution within the assigned scope.
  • Complete documentation, follow-up, escalation, and information accuracy.
  • Operational excellence and measurable improvement within the assigned team or area.
  • Department or wider business outcomes for roles with broader scope.

The underlying work remains broadly consistent across levels; what changes is the scope of ownership, complexity, judgement, influence, and impact expected.

What We Value

  • Strong ownership, accuracy, and documentation discipline.
  • Customer-centric thinking and professional communication.
  • Structured problem-solving and reliable follow-through until task completion.
  • Integrity, confidentiality, and disciplined handling of customer and operational information.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced digital environment.
  • Cross-functional collaboration and continuous improvement mindset.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Demonstrated independent ownership of complex work, cross-functional influence and measurable improvement.
  • Strong analytical judgement, risk awareness and structured problem solving.
  • Relevant experience in customer service, operations, finance, payments, cashiering, business support, or a related digital operational environment is preferred.
  • Strong written and spoken English with professional and customer-focused communication.
  • Strong attention to detail, documentation discipline, and ability to manage multiple priorities.
  • Ability to analyse operational information, identify issues or risks, and coordinate appropriate action.
  • Ability to work independently and remain accountable for tasks through completion.
  • Ability to protect confidential information and follow service policies, data-protection standards, and internal procedures.

Candidates are assessed based on demonstrated capability, ownership, and impact rather than previous job title alone.

Management Track Add-On

As a Management Track role, the following people-leadership accountabilities apply:

  • Own team performance, service levels, quality, documentation standards, and operational output.
  • Run team operating rhythms, workload allocation, coaching, and capability development.
  • Own escalations and ensure timely response to material payment-service or customer issues.
  • Support hiring, onboarding, performance management, and development of team members.
