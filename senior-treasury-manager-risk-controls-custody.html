Role Overview

Senior Treasury Manager role owning treasury risk, internal controls and custody operations. Fully remote. You own the day-to-day safety of fiat and digital-asset holdings: control design, custody operations and audit readiness, and manage the team that carries this out.

Key Responsibilities

Risk and controls framework

  • Design, document and maintain treasury controls covering counterparty risk, FX risk, custody risk and operational risk.
  • Identify control gaps and coordinate remediation with Finance, Legal and Compliance.

Custody operations

  • Own day-to-day custody operations for fiat and digital assets: wallet and key management, multi-signature approvals, and oversight of custodians and exchanges.
  • Monitor custody and settlement activity for anomalies, and escalate issues promptly.

Audit and compliance

  • Prepare treasury controls and custody documentation for internal and external audit.
  • Track open audit items on treasury risk and custody through to closure.

Process improvement and team leadership

  • Improve treasury control and custody processes, reducing manual risk and single points of failure.
  • Plan, assign and supervise the team's work; coach team members and give timely feedback.

What You Will Be Accountable For

  • Effectiveness of the treasury risk and controls framework.
  • Security and accuracy of custody operations for fiat and digital assets.
  • Audit readiness for treasury controls and custody.
  • Team performance, service quality and development.

What We Value

Ownership of outcomes. Independence without supervision. Analytical rigour and control and risk awareness. Evidence-based recommendations. Everyday use of AI at work. Clear communication with stakeholders in a fully remote setting.

Qualifications and Requirements

  • Demonstrated experience owning treasury risk, internal controls or custody operations, with direct team leadership.
  • Experience with custody of digital-asset treasury operations is required.
  • Strong understanding of internal control frameworks and audit processes.
  • Works without supervision and uses AI in day-to-day work.
  • Professional qualification (CTP, CIA, ACT or equivalent) is advantageous.
  • Fluent business English.

Management Track Add-On

  • 2+ years of direct team management, including hiring, performance and day-to-day direction.
  • Set team priorities, run operating rhythms (1-on-1s, reviews), and own team outcomes.

Work Set-up

Fully remote. Monday to Friday, 10:00am–7:00pm GMT+8.
