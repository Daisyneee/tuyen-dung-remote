Special Projects – Strategy & Operations (Multiple Levels)

Department: Special Projects. Reports directly to the Special Projects Owner or Department Head. Grade: multiple levels, Expert or Management Track.

Level expectation

Level is determined by the scope of outcomes the candidate has demonstrated, from individual work outcomes through to company-wide business outcomes.

Role overview

The Special Projects – Strategy & Operations role contributes to cross-functional project delivery from scoping through implementation. The role works across different business problems, projects and departments, depending on business needs. Responsibilities may include project planning, execution and coordination, analysis and recommendations, and implementation and learning.

This role may be appointed at different Job Grades depending on the candidate's demonstrated capability, ownership and level of business impact. Final title, grade and career track will be confirmed during the hiring process. For Expert Track roles, formal people management is not an inherent responsibility. For Management Track roles, formal people-management accountability applies.

Key responsibilities

Project planning

  • Translate approved objectives into workplans, milestones and expected outcomes.
  • Maintain project documentation, dependencies, risks and status.
  • Prepare clear and decision-ready project updates.
  • Identify issues early and recommend practical next steps.

Execution and coordination

  • Coordinate workstreams and stakeholder actions.
  • Track deliverables and follow up on commitments.
  • Resolve delivery blockers within assigned authority.
  • Escalate material issues when additional support or decisions are required.

Analysis and recommendations

  • Gather and structure business information.
  • Analyse options, risks, root causes and implementation implications.
  • Develop clear and evidence-based recommendations.
  • Support decision-making by presenting practical options and trade-offs.

Implementation and learning

  • Support pilots, launches and implementation activities.
  • Monitor adoption, progress and early outcomes.
  • Capture lessons and identify areas for improvement.
  • Improve delivery methods, processes or ways of working where appropriate.

What you will be accountable for

Accountability varies by Job Grade and Career Track. Depending on level, this may include: accurate and timely completion of assigned work; quality and results across an assigned area; department-level standards, decisions or outcomes; multi-department business outcomes; company-wide or long-term business outcomes; and effective delivery across project planning, execution and coordination, analysis and recommendations, and implementation and learning. The underlying work remains broadly consistent across levels. What changes is the scope of ownership, judgement and influence expected.

What we value

Structured thinking. Bias for execution. Stakeholder influence. Comfort with ambiguity. Ownership, adaptability and continuous improvement. Ability to learn quickly and operate across unfamiliar areas. Credible influence through evidence, judgement and recommendations. Focus on practical business impact.

Qualifications and requirements

  • Relevant project, strategy, operations, business improvement, commercial, product, technology or cross-functional experience.
  • Strong analytical and presentation skills.
  • Ability to structure unclear problems and develop practical recommendations.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities remotely.
  • Ability to work across different teams and business areas.
  • Bachelor's degree in Business, Management, Finance or a related field is preferred but not mandatory.

We assess candidates based on demonstrated capability, ownership and impact rather than previous job title alone.

Management Track add-on

Where the role includes formal people responsibility, the following also apply.

  • 2+ years managing a team directly, including hiring, performance and day-to-day direction.
  • Has owned team output and been accountable for the results of others.
  • Has hired and onboarded at least one team member end to end (preferred).
  • Has managed underperformance through coaching, performance management or exit where required.

Added responsibilities: own team performance, targets, quality and output; run team operating rhythms including 1-on-1s, team reviews and workload allocation; develop team members, close capability gaps and build future successors; own escalations when the team is blocked; support hiring and interviewer calibration for the team.
